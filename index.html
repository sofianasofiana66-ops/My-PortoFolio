My Story

I am a 10th grade student in a vocational high school majoring in Software Engineering (RPL). At first, I felt nervous because everything was new for me. I did not know much about programming or website development, but I was very excited to learn new things.

In school, I started learning basic programming such as HTML and CSS. Sometimes I struggled when writing code because my website did not always work correctly. However, I kept practicing and asked my teacher or friends for help whenever I had difficulties.

I also watched tutorials on YouTube and practiced coding at home. One of my best experiences was creating my first simple website. Even though the design was simple, I felt very proud because I made it by myself.

Now, I feel more confident and enjoy learning programming. I want to continue improving my skills and become a professional frontend developer in the future.
